How to Play

  1. Spin the wheel first to start your turn.
  2. Guess one consonant. If it's in the puzzle, your score increases by wedge value × number of that letter.
  3. If the consonant isn't there, your turn passes to the next contestant.
  4. Want to buy a vowel? It costs 10 points (only after the wheel has been spun).
  5. BANKRUPT — lose your turn and your score resets to 0.
  6. LOSE A TURN — turn passes, score unchanged.
  7. FREE SPIN — get one free spin after a correct consonant guess.
  8. SURPRISE / GIFT BOX — an extra prize, but only if you solve the round.
  9. JACKPOT — a big prize; solve the puzzle immediately to claim it.
  10. You have 5 seconds to think. Solve the full puzzle with SOLVE PUZZLE to win!

🧬 About the questions: 25 puzzles, all about STEM careers — jobs in Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics, and Health & Medicine.
